# Privacy Policy — kolophon

_Last updated: 17 June 2026_

kolophon is a browser extension that lets you inspect, copy, and edit the fonts
on any web page. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do
with your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing is sent to us or to any server we control.** kolophon has no backend,
no analytics, no tracking, and no account system.

## Data stored on your device

When you save a font to your **collection**, the font's details (family, weight,
size, color, and the host name of the page you found it on) are stored locally
in your browser using the standard `storage` API. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is not transmitted to us or any third party,
- can be removed at any time by deleting items from your collection or
  uninstalling the extension.

## Page content

When you activate the inspector on a page, kolophon reads the computed styles of
the elements you hover or click (font family, size, color, etc.) so it can show
and copy them. This happens entirely in your browser, only on the tab where you
invoked the extension, and only while the inspector is active. None of it is
recorded or transmitted.

## Third-party requests

To preview saved and inspected fonts in their real typeface, the side panel may
request font files from **Google Fonts** (`fonts.googleapis.com` /
`fonts.gstatic.com`). These requests are made by your browser directly to Google
and are subject to
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Only the font
family name is included in the request; no personal data is sent. Fonts that are
not available on Google Fonts simply fall back to a system typeface.

## Permissions

kolophon requests the minimum permissions needed to function: access to the
active tab only when you click its icon, on-demand script injection, local
storage for your collection, clipboard write for copying, and the side panel.
It does not request access to all websites.
